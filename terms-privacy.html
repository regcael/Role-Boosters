Retainer

The Retainer provides ongoing implementation support within the agreed Role Booster scope for the applicable monthly service period. It covers only the tasks, deliverables, platforms and frequency specifically agreed with the Client, and does not create an unlimited or open-ended work arrangement.

The Retainer is an ongoing part of the Booster service. The Booster operates only while the Retainer is active and paid. If the Retainer ends or is not paid, the Booster stops from the end of the current paid month, and access to it is withdrawn. Restarting is a new booking and will require a new setup fee.

The Retainer is invoiced on the 15th of the previous month, and payment is due by the 25th. Work for a new service period begins only after payment is received and confirmed by the Service Provider’s bank. If payment is not received by the 25th, the Retainer stops for the following month and resumes only after payment is received and confirmed.

An update request is one round of changes to existing work, sent by email. Each active Booster includes one update request per month. Unused work, time, capacity or update requests do not carry forward.

Requests outside the agreed Retainer scope, including additional platforms, substantially increased volume, new categories of work or work belonging to another Role Booster, will be quoted separately.

A Retainer does not guarantee any particular increase in enquiries, sales, followers, engagement, reach, productivity, revenue or other business outcome.
